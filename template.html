Digital Twin Design Landscape

Digital twins and related systems in chemistry, materials science, and quantum information science, scored on six design axes. Choose what each plot dimension shows, compare fingerprints, and place your own system on the map.

Explore the landscape

Hover a point for its scores; click a point or a table row to show its fingerprint. Axis 4 is a base-10 logarithm.

What each axis measures and what each level means, from the rubric. Scores between two levels are intermediate values. Full rubric and decision rules.

Place your system

Your system is drawn as a star on the plot and added to the table. Nothing you enter here leaves your browser.

Enter scores

Score each axis with the rubric, then add the system to the map.

Load scores from the scoring skill

The scoring skill reads a manuscript and returns a JSON score file. Load that file, or paste its contents, to place the system.

  1. Download the scoring skill and add it to Claude.
  2. Upload your manuscript to Claude and ask it to score the system.
  3. Load the JSON file it returns here.

Submit for review

Submit a system you added so it appears for everyone. It is listed as pending until the maintainers check the scores against the source paper, then marked as verified.

"Review and submit" opens a page that summarizes your system, checks that every field is complete, and prepares a GitHub issue. Include the DOI and the evidence and basis (E/I/T) for each score that the skill returns. To propose a new axis or a change to an existing one, use "Suggest new/modify axis" beside the fingerprint.

Scores follow rubric v (Appendix A of Grieder and Qian, “What Makes a Digital Twin?”). Rationales, source evidence, and contribution guidelines are in the open rubric repository. Read the current rubric and decision rules.
